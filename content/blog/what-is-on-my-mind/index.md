I have enjoyed working on several projects, both at the office and at home. Here are the personal projects occupying my mind.

## Projects

- Continue building the Django Facebook Album plugin.
- Improve my Twitter-like Node.js application.
- Explore a fresh open source idea instead of collecting unfinished experiments.

## Learning

- Read more source code from projects I respect.
- Strengthen my understanding of JavaScript, Python, databases, algorithms, and testing.
- Write down what I learn so the knowledge does not disappear after the immediate task.

## Working habits

- Plan the next day the night before.
- Give incoming issues and requests an explicit priority.
- Finish a small number of meaningful tasks before starting new ones.
- Protect time from arguments, social media, and conversations that add neither value nor rest.

The list is simple, but that is the point. I do not need more ambitions; I need enough focus to turn the important ones into finished work.
